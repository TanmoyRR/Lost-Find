About Us

About IUBAT SmartFind

Dedicated to reuniting students and staff with their lost belongings through technology and community spirit.

Our Mission

To provide a reliable, AI-powered platform that makes it easy for the IUBAT community to report lost and found items, increasing the chances of reuniting people with their valuables while fostering a culture of honesty and helpfulness on campus.

Our Vision

To become the standard for lost and found management across universities in Bangladesh, leveraging artificial intelligence to create smarter, faster, and more effective item recovery for everyone.

Our Story

IUBAT SmartFind was born from a simple observation: every semester, countless valuable items go unclaimed because there was no centralized system to connect those who lost items with those who found them.

What started as a student project quickly grew into a campus-wide initiative. With the support of the university administration and the enthusiasm of the student body, we developed a platform that combines traditional lost and found reporting with cutting-edge AI semantic matching technology.

Today, our platform serves thousands of IUBAT students and staff, helping reunite people with their lost phones, wallets, books, ID cards, and more. Every successful reunion is a testament to the power of community and technology working together.
